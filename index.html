Evolution of the Ecosystem: Transitioning to Wrapped Coinflect

We are excited to announce the official release of the Wrapped Coinflect protocol upgrade. This transition brings full ERC-20 compatibility, lower gas overhead, and unified liquidity access across major decentralized finance (DeFi) platforms.

✓ Key Migration Highlights
  • Exact 1:1 Swap Ratio: Every 1 Coinflect (v1) converts directly to 1 Wrapped Coinflect (wCFLT).
  • Zero Slippage & Fee Offset: Protocol handles wrapper fees with zero price impact.
  • Cross-Chain Interoperability: Built on audited CCIP contracts for seamless multi-chain routing.

Why Wrap Coinflect?

The legacy Coinflect architecture lacked standard token callbacks required by modern automated market makers (AMMs) and lending vaults. Wrapped Coinflect encapsulates native utility into an ERC-20 standard, unlocking staking rewards, cross-chain bridges, and decentralized governance voting without compromising key security parameters.
